Qubits

The fundamental unit of quantum information, capable of existing as a linear superposition of computational basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩.

α+β

Superposition

The physical phenomenon enabling a quantum system to represent multiple state configurations simultaneously until measurement collapses the wavefunction.

U

Quantum Gates

Reversible mathematical operators that act on qubits to perform state rotations while conserving the total probability sum (norm preservation).

X·Y·Z

Pauli Gates

Core single-qubit rotation operators: Pauli-X performs bit flips, Pauli-Z alters phase by π radians, and Pauli-Y combines bit and phase alterations.

H

Hadamard Gate

Maps basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩ into equal superposition states (|+⟩ and |−⟩), creating the fundamental quantum state interference needed in algorithms.

U†U=I

Unitary Gates

Transformations represented by complex square matrices whose conjugate transpose equals their inverse, guaranteeing deterministically reversible computation.

T₂

Quantum Decoherence

The gradual loss of quantum state coherence due to thermal noise and environmental interaction, shifting quantum superpositions into classical probabilistic states.

QSVM

QML & QSVM

Exploring Quantum Machine Learning and Quantum Support Vector Machines, which map non-linear classical data into exponentially large Hilbert spaces for classification.
